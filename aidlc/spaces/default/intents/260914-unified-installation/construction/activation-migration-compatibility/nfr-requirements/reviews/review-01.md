### Summary

The quantified answers the human agreed to are honoured faithfully — 2 s p95 non-interactive, no activation-time wait for overwrite input, non-modal warning plus Output-channel detail with credentials redacted — and ID-level traceability is clean and bidirectional. What blocks READY is the consequence of that budget decision being left unstated: with no activation-time wait, the functional spec's `confirmed` overwrite branch and Contract 4's required `MigrationInteractionPort` have no defined behaviour (R-01), the cancellation half of the contract question this stage was assigned is unanswered (R-02), and the destructive path's contractual token/rollback controls are absent from the one artifact that should carry them (R-05).

Gate input rather than defects: security-requirements.md has no authentication, authorization, or compliance sections — reasonable for a local-filesystem compatibility library, but worth an explicit one-line "not applicable, and why" so the omission reads as a decision; and a short threat-consideration note on the bounded exception that lets U4 read another extension's internal storage (functional-spec.md § Boundary) would strengthen the artifact without changing any target.
